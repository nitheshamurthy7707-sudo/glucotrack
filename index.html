Learn the Science 📚

A simple introduction to dietary carbohydrates and blood glucose response.

🍚 Dietary Carbohydrates


  • Carbohydrates are major energy-providing nutrients.
  • They include sugars, starches and dietary fibre.
  • Digestible carbohydrates can be broken down into smaller sugars.
  • Glucose can then be absorbed into the bloodstream.
  • Different foods can produce different glucose responses.

🔬 Food → Digestion → Glucose


  • Food enters the digestive system.
  • Mechanical and chemical digestion break food down.
  • Carbohydrates are converted into simpler sugars.
  • Glucose is absorbed mainly in the small intestine.
  • The body regulates blood glucose using hormones and tissues.
